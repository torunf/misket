Findle — Privacy

Last updated: 16 August 2026

The short version: Findle collects no data.

Where your data lives

Findle reads only file names, sizes and dates; it never opens file contents. The index is stored only on your Mac; there is no network access, no account and no analytics.

What we don't collect

  • No account, email address or phone number.
  • No analytics, crash reporting or advertising identifiers.
  • No third-party tracking SDKs.
  • We have no server; there is nowhere for your data to go.

Purchases

Any in-app purchase is handled by Apple through the App Store; we never see your payment details. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

Children

We collect no information from anyone, children included, because we collect none at all.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version appears on this page with an updated date.
